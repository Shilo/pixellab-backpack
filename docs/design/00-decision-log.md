# 00 — Decision Log: PixelLab Backpack (formerly Vitrine)

Every decision behind the design docs, in one place. Status 2026-10-21 (simplicity redesign S-1 to S-21; MVP and product rulings P-1 to P-21). One line each; the linked doc holds the detail. Older rows say "Vitrine", the working name: read it as PixelLab Backpack (P-15).

**Precedence (S-1, amending B-5; the canonical statement, other docs point here):**
1. The P rulings that are not marked superseded ([MVP plan](../plan/mvp-plan.md); §7 below). P-1 to P-12 were written for the MVP; P-16 makes the API rules among them permanent, and P-13 onward hold for the whole product. Then the redesign rulings S-1 to S-21, then the final rulings F-1 to F-16 and the builder answers B-1 to B-20 that are not marked superseded. Together they override any conflicting text anywhere.
2. For a feature a phase plan built, that plan's rulings tables (`docs/plan/phaseN-plan.md`) override 02, 05, 07 and 08. The rows of this log still override the phase plans.
3. Then 08 (layout, visual structure and controls), 02 (behaviour) and 07 (token values and visual accessibility invariants only). Where 08 differs from 02 or 07, 08 wins.
4. Then 01, 03, 05 and 06.
5. Then the simple-concept mockups (`mockups/simple/1-just-the-art/`, plus the parts of `mockups/simple/2-home-screen/` that 08 borrows; kept locally, not committed). They illustrate 08 and never override it: a detail that exists only in a mockup goes into 08 or 02 before it is built.
6. Finally the earlier explorations: 04, the option mockups A, B and C, `mockups/recommended/` and `mockups/simple/3-library-tiles/` (historical; kept locally, not committed).

**ID families:** P = MVP plan and product rulings (from 2026-10-19; `docs/plan/mvp-plan.md` and the 2026-10-21 product review) · S = simplicity-redesign rulings (2026-10-19; design 08) · D = R1 research cross-review · R = earlier orchestrator rulings · F = final orchestrator rulings · B = orchestrator answers to the R4 builder check · E = editor calls made while applying the F rulings · UX / AR = builder-relevant **(decision)** items in 02 / 05.

**Cross-references (B-18):** design docs cite research documents as "research NN" (`docs/research/NN-…`) and spell out "design 07" or "research 07" wherever both could be meant. A bare "07 Qn" is never written; the Qn questions are research 07's.
