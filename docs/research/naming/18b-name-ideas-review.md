# Name review: an independent second pass

Temporary notes (git-ignored). I built and scored this list from scratch. I used the earlier draft (`name-ideas.md`) only as a source of facts, after checking a few of them. This is not legal advice.

## Facts checked

- **PixelLab's own product names** are PixelLab Editor, PixelLab Game Builder, PixelLab Map Workshop, PixelLab MCP server and PixelLab API. The site has no gallery, library or collection page today, so a plain function word would look like the next official product. Its in-app pages use names like "My effects".
- **Backpack** is an active crypto wallet and exchange app on the App Store ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/backpack-crypto-wallet/id6445964121)). Confirmed.
- **Pixel Shelf** is a Chrome extension for saving images ([Hunted](https://www.hunted.space/product/pixel-shelf)). Confirmed, but it is tiny: third-party trackers count about 2 users ([extensionauditor](https://extensionauditor.com/scan/pixel-shelf-image-saver-bookmarker-dddllcliaipgobfbbljiabmkakohdjjm)).
- **Satchel One** is a UK school homework platform. Confirmed.
- **Trove:** I found no app called "Pixel Trove". Other Trove apps exist: an idea-saving app on iPhone, and a Microsoft Garage experiment that has since been retired. Trove is also a voxel MMO game, which game developers may know.
- **New finding the draft missed:** several card-collection apps are already called "Binder", for sports cards, Pokémon and MTG ([example](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/binder-sports-card-tracker/id6742280637)). That's a naming conflict, but it also confirms that "binder" means "my collection".
- **Spritebook:** no conflict found.

## Rubric

I set these weights before scoring. Each criterion is scored from 1 to 5, where 5 is best.

| Criterion | Weight | What a 5 means |
|---|---|---|
| **C: Clarity** | 25 | A new user guesses "a place to see and keep my own art" from the name alone |
| **D: Distinct from official** | 20 | Nobody would think PixelLab made it |
| **M: Memorable and easy to say** | 15 | Common word that's easy to spell, including for people who don't speak English natively |
| **T: Tone** | 15 | Fits a calm, minimal, iOS-like app for game artists |
| **R: Conflict risk** | 10 | No well-known product shares the word in apps, art or game dev |
| **G: Game-dev meaning** | 10 | The word has no technical meaning in game dev that would confuse people |
| **B: Brevity** | 5 | Second word only: up to 5 letters scores 5, 6 scores 4, 7 scores 3, 8 scores 2, 9 or more scores 1 |

**Score:** total = sum of (score × weight) ÷ sum of weights × 20, which gives a number from 0 to 100.

**Hard-rule gate:** a name with D ≤ 2 fails the "must not look official" rule, no matter what it totals. I still scored the gated names so you can compare them, and I mark them with ✗.

**Why some scores are low:**

## Challenging the earlier #1 (PixelLab Backpack)

**The strongest case against Backpack:**

- The draft ranked mainly by one criterion: being unlikely to look official. On the criterion that matters most to new users, clarity, Backpack is mid-pack. It means "storage you carry", not "a gallery where you view and play animations". The draft admitted that the subtitle has to carry the word "gallery".
- Its name neighbor is the Backpack crypto wallet and exchange, which is active in the same app stores. A tool for downloading your account's assets doesn't want to share a name with a wallet.
- Its tone is playful and schoolbag-like, not calm and minimal.
- At 8 letters, it is the longest of the top names.
- Its main precedent, backpack.tf, is a niche site for one game (Team Fortress 2).
- Under five different weight sets it ranks 4th to 13th, with a median of 7th.

**The strongest case for its rivals:**

- **Shelf** is the most natural "display and keep" word, fits a minimal iOS-style app best (think Books bookshelf), is easy for everyone to say, is 5 letters, and has only a tiny 2-user extension as a neighbor. It wins under every weight set.
- **Trove** matches Backpack's "clearly not official" score, says "valued collection" more directly, and is shorter.
- **Binder** fits the folders and favorites better than any other name.

**Verdict: Backpack does not deserve #1.** It is a solid, safe name, but a top-5-to-7 pick, not the winner. It is also beaten when distinctiveness gets the most weight, which is the one view that favors it.

## Final top 5

1. **PixelLab Shelf**: the clearest calm "see and keep your art" word. It ranks first under every weight set, is 5 letters, and has no meaningful conflict.
2. **PixelLab Trove**: the most clearly unofficial collection word. It is the fallback if Shelf feels too much like a feature name, though some non-native speakers won't know it.
3. **PixelLab Museum**: a universal "place to view art" word with no conflicts. Its weakness is that it suggests a public showcase rather than your own files.
4. **PixelLab Folio**: elegant and short, with a strong tone. It slips under some weights because it is less clearly unofficial and less familiar.
5. **PixelLab Binder**: the best match for organizing with folders and favorites, and clearly fan-made. It is a bit office-like, and card-collection apps already use the name. It ties at 75 with Backpack, Stash and Spritebook, and wins the tie on the first-impression test.

**#1: PixelLab Shelf.** Set the home-screen `short_name` to "Shelf", give the app its own shelf icon, use the subtitle "an unofficial gallery for your PixelLab art", and add a "Not made by PixelLab" footer. The draft's guidance on the brand prefix (ask PixelLab before any App Store or Play release, and keep "Shelf for PixelLab" as the fallback) still applies.
